# Junteng Liu

I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the **HKUST NLP Group**, **Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)**, supervised by **Professor Junxian He**. My research focuses on **natural language processing (NLP)** and **machine learning (ML)**.

## Academic Background

- **Ph.D. in Computer Science (2024 - Present)**
  - Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)
  - Member of the **HKUST NLP Group**, led by Professor Junxian He
- **B.Eng. (2020 - 2024)**
  - Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)
  - Graduated in June 2024; previously advised by Professor Junxian He during undergraduate studies at SJTU
- **Honors & Awards**
  - Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship, Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)

## Research Experience

- **Research Intern at MINIMAX** (February 2025 - Present)
- **Research Intern at Tencent WXG** (June 2024 - September 2024)
  - Division of Tencent technology company; Advisor: Zifei Shan
- **Research Intern at Shanghai AI Lab** (June 2023 - December 2023)
  - AI research laboratory; Advisor: Prof. Yu Cheng

## Skills & Research Interests

- Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Machine Learning (ML)
- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
